SECOND PRIORITY

Music development

Build practical musical competence and a growing repertoire. Detailed guitar and music-training lessons will be added in a later learning phase.

Piano

Daily structured practice. Curriculum to be developed: technique, scales, chords, inversions, progressions, ear training, accompaniment and songs.

Guitar · beginner

Start from novice foundations: instrument familiarity, tuning, posture, basic chords, transitions, rhythm, strumming and complete songs. Step-by-step course to follow later.

Voice and singing

Breathing, pitch accuracy, tone, resonance, range, vocal control, ear training and singing complete songs. Train gently; stop if there is pain or persistent hoarseness.

Hymns, worship and praise

Learn lyrics, melody and meaning. Practise singing without relying on a screen. Understand biblical foundations and develop song-leading and musical flow.

Song and hymn repertoire

Track lyrics, melody, meaning and independent performance separately.
